      About the data

      We mine prompts from the LaTeX sources of arXiv cs.SE papers (primary or secondary category) first submitted from January 2023 to October 2026.

      We plan to add PDF-only papers, SE venues beyond arXiv, and prompts shown only as images or in replication packages.

      Reading the map

      Each dot is a prompt, placed by a 2D UMAP projection of its Harrier-OSS-v1-0.6B embedding, so nearby prompts are similar. Task types come from clustering these embeddings with UMAP and HDBSCAN, followed by manual screening.

        About us

        The people and plans behind SEPIA

        SEPIA (Software Engineering Prompts In Articles) collects the prompts that software engineering researchers report in their papers, so that they can be found, compared and reused.

        What we plan next

        Wider CoverageMore of the prompts that SE research reports
        • PDF-only papers

          Extract prompts from the cs.SE papers that are on arXiv only as PDF.

        • SE venues beyond arXiv

          Cover papers from software engineering venues that are not posted on arXiv.

        • Images and replication packages

          Capture the prompts that papers show only as figures or keep in their artifacts.

        Richer LabelsA clearer picture of every prompt
        • Prompting techniques

          Annotate the techniques the prompts use, such as few-shot examples or chain-of-thought instructions.

        • Better clustering

          Improve how prompts are grouped into task types, so that fewer of them stay unassigned.

        • More typed prompts

          Validate the suggested task types for unassigned prompts and fold the reliable ones into the labels.
